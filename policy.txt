MANGOBATTERY PRIVACY POLICY
Effective date: October 1, 2026

1. Scope and publisher

This policy explains how MangoBattery, published by Dmitry Porotnikov,
handles information. It applies to the MangoBattery Windows application.

MangoBattery displays battery information on your computer. It does not
require an account, and its battery-monitoring functionality works offline.
The app does not send the information it reads to the publisher or to an
external service.

2. Information accessed on your device

While running, MangoBattery reads information provided by Windows:

- Your computer name, which may contain a personal name or another identifier.
- Battery instance names or identifiers reported by Windows.
- Battery charge, remaining capacity, design capacity, full-charge capacity,
  cycle count, voltage, charge and discharge rates, charging status, power
  connection status, and battery active or critical status.
- The local date and time used to label the displayed snapshot.
- Error details when Windows cannot provide battery information.

The app uses Windows Management Instrumentation (WMI) to read local battery
data. These queries retrieve records from the BatteryStatus,
BatteryFullChargedCapacity, BatteryStaticData, and BatteryCycleCount classes.
Windows may return additional hardware properties as part of those records;
the app uses the fields needed for the battery display.

This information is processed locally to display current battery statistics,
calculate charge and health percentages, identify batteries, and explain
errors. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or identifying users to
the publisher. MangoBattery does not access your documents, contacts,
messages, browsing history, precise location, microphone, or camera as part
of its functionality.

3. Storage, retention, and security

MangoBattery holds the information it reads in application memory while
running. It does not create a persistent battery history, save these readings
to files or a database, or maintain a server-side copy. Closing the app stops
its monitoring and ends its use of this in-memory information.

Local information is subject to Windows security and your device's access
controls. The app does not provide a separate encrypted data store. Windows
may independently manage paging, diagnostic records, or crash reports under
its own settings and policies.

4. Transmission, sharing, and tracking

MangoBattery contains no advertising, analytics, tracking, or app-managed
crash-reporting service. It does not upload battery readings, computer names,
or battery identifiers. The publisher does not receive, sell, rent, or share
those readings through the app. There are no in-app cookies or advertising
identifiers.

Microsoft independently operates Windows and the Microsoft Store, including
installation, updates, and any platform diagnostics. This policy does not
govern Microsoft's handling of information through those services. See the
Microsoft Privacy Statement: https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement

5. Desktop permission

The Store package declares the runFullTrust capability for its Windows
desktop execution model and WMI-based battery functionality. This does not
mean the app requests administrator elevation. MangoBattery uses read-only
battery queries and does not modify battery or system settings.

6. Your choices and access

You can view the information the app uses in its window. Close the app to
stop its queries, or uninstall it through Windows Settings to remove it.
There is no MangoBattery account or stored battery history to delete, and
no app-level data transmission to opt out of. Uninstalling the app does not
delete battery information maintained independently by Windows or hardware.

Screenshots can reveal your computer name and battery identifiers. Remove
these details before sharing a screenshot if you do not want to disclose
them. The app does not automatically share screenshots.

7. Questions and information you voluntarily provide

For privacy questions, contact Dmitry Porotnikov through the project's issue
tracker: https://github.com/dmitryporotnikov/MangoBattery/issues

GitHub issues are public. Do not post passwords, sensitive information, or
unredacted device details. If you choose to submit an issue, the publisher
can see your GitHub username and the content you provide and will use that
information to respond to the request or investigate the reported problem.
It may remain in the issue history until edited or removed, subject to
GitHub's controls and retention practices. You can request removal of
information under the publisher's control through the same contact route.

GitHub processes information under its own privacy statement:
https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, or
delete personal information you voluntarily provide, or to object to or
restrict its processing. Use the contact route above to make a request
without publicly posting additional personal information. The publisher
does not hold a remotely accessible copy of your app's local battery data.

8. Children

The app does not request age information or collect personal information
from children through an account, advertising, or online tracking. The local
processing described in this policy applies to all users.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated if the app's information practices change. The
effective date above identifies the latest revision. Updated versions will
be made available through the app's published privacy-policy location.
